The Story
How Analytica Started

In 2026, a group of young Nigerian data analysts noticed something frustrating — every resource they used was built for someone else. The datasets were about American cities. The job boards listed only London and New York roles. The communities didn't understand Nigerian business contexts.

So they decided to build the platform they wished existed. One that had Nigerian Bureau of Statistics data. One that listed jobs at Flutterwave and MTN. One where you could ask questions about WAEC data or CBN reports and get real answers from people who understood.

That platform is Analytica. Built in Nigeria. For Africa. And now growing across the continent — from Lagos to Nairobi, Accra to Johannesburg.
