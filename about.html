🚀 Our Story

Built by Tech People,
For Tech People.

Analytica was born from a simple frustration — brilliant African tech talent had no single platform designed for them. So we built one. And then we made it for the world.

How It Started

Three Friends.
One Big Problem.

It started in a WhatsApp group. Three young Nigerians — a React developer, a full stack engineer, and a data analyst — were frustrated with the same things. Job boards that ignored Africa. Platforms that made collaboration impossible. Portfolios nobody could see. Learning resources that assumed you had a $100/month budget.

The Problem We Saw

Talented developers across Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and the rest of Africa were building incredible things — but in isolation. No platform existed that truly understood what a Nigerian developer needed: local context, global reach, affordable tools, and a community that looked like them. We watched friends take loans to pay for Coursera. We watched brilliant engineers get ignored by job boards because they had ".ng" email addresses. We watched startups struggle to find co-founders because there was no collab space built for us.

The Decision We Made

In late 2025, Mishael, Frank and Ayomide made a decision that changed everything — stop complaining and start building. Working evenings after their day jobs, fueled by jollof rice and determination, they began coding the platform they wished existed. No VC funding. No fancy office. Just three laptops, one shared Figma board, and an obsession with building something that would actually matter to African tech people — and eventually, to tech people everywhere.

Our Mission

We Exist to Level the Playing Field

Every tech person on earth — regardless of where they were born, what university they attended, or how much money they have — deserves access to world-class tools, opportunities and community. That is not a nice-to-have. That is our reason for existing.

Our Journey

From WhatsApp Group
to Global Platform

💬
August 2025
The WhatsApp Conversation That Started Everything
Mishael, Frank and Ayomide are complaining in a group chat about Nigerian developers being invisible on global platforms. Someone says "why don't we just build our own?" Nobody laughs.
📝
September 2025
First Lines of Code
Mishael sketches the first wireframes. Frank sets up the Supabase backend. Ayomide writes the business plan on his phone during his lunch break. Analytica officially begins.
🚀
November 2025
First 100 Users
They share the beta link in their university WhatsApp groups. 100 signups in 48 hours. The servers survive. The team does not sleep for two days.
🌍
January 2026
Official Public Launch
Analytica launches publicly with Jobs, Challenges, Collab Rooms and Community. First users from 14 countries sign up on day one. The mission goes global.
⭐
Today
120,000+ Members and Growing
Analytica is now used by developers, designers, data scientists and founders across 80+ countries. We are just getting started.
